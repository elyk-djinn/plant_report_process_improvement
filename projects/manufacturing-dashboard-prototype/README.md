# Manufacturing plant dashboard prototype (2020 to 2024)

An Excel dashboard prototype dated June 23, 2025, built on a 60-month sample dataset. It shows how a clean data table, pivot tables and slicers can drive a full dashboard without hand-built summaries.

## What is in the workbook

- `Data`: a structured table with five calculated columns (gross profit, profit margin, defect rate, return rate, downtime %), so new rows calculate themselves
- `Pivot Tables` and `Pivot Tables 2`: 17 pivot tables that feed every chart and card
- `Dashboard`: 11 charts and KPI cards with year slicers, including revenue vs operating expense, margin trend, production output, downtime, defect rate, and a radar of service quality, responsiveness, customer feedback and on-time delivery

## Observations from the sample data

- Complaints fell from 213 in 2020 to 58 in 2024, and average delivery lead time dropped from 4.1 to 2.8 days.
- Gross margin rose from 34.5% in 2022 to 41.1% in 2023 because operating expense fell while revenue was nearly flat.
- 2024 shows a reversal: revenue fell about 11% from the 2023 peak, defect rate rose from 2.16% (2022) to 2.53%, and downtime went from 88 to 119 hours.

The data is illustrative.
